    How to Play Countryle

    Goal: Figure out the mystery country before time runs out. Comment the country's name in the TikTok live chat to guess — first correct guess wins the round.

    The globe: every guess lights up gold on the floating globe above the board, so viewers can see where guesses are landing. Drag it to look around, or zoom in on a region.

    Reading your feedback tags:

    • 🌍 continent match the target is on the same continent as your guess
    • 👥 pop ▲/▼ the target's population is higher / lower than your guess
    • 📐 area ▲/▼ the target's land area is bigger / smaller than your guess
    • ↗ NE · 7,533 km compass direction & distance from your guess to the target
    • 62% close an overall "hot or cold" score — greener means closer, 100% means correct

    Sorting: every guess stays on screen for the whole round and automatically re-sorts, closest to the answer always rising to the top.

    Hints: the host can reveal up to 3 hints (continent → first letter → capital city) from the 💡 button. Each hint used slightly lowers the points available that round.

    Difficulty: changes how long each round lasts — Easy gives the most time, Expert the least.

    Scoring: correct guesses earn points, with a bonus for being first. "This Round" resets every round; "All-Time" adds up across the whole stream.
